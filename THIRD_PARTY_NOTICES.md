The build preserves the original libav.js factory and its full license block. It narrows one WebAssembly memory declaration. The exact transformation and original hashes are in `packages/core/build-audio.mjs`. Ship the corresponding upstream codec sources, configuration, dependency versions, notices and this modification with a binary distribution. Preserve a practical way for recipients to rebuild or replace the separate library. An upstream link alone is insufficient as the release's corresponding-source package. Source checkout and debug compilation are the currently supported distribution paths.
